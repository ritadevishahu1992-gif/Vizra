# VIZRA AI Creative Studio — Crawler & AI Bot Access Policy
# Canonical Host: https://vizra.vercel.app
#
# Policy Design:
#   1. All genuine public marketing, product, documentation, showcase, pricing,
#      and legal routes plus /llms.txt, /llms-full.txt, /sitemap.xml, and
#      /og-image.png are explicitly OPEN to search engines and AI search/retrieval
#      bots (Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot,
#      Claude-SearchBot, Applebot).
#   2. AI foundation/training crawlers (GPTBot, Google-Extended, ClaudeBot) are
#      likewise permitted across VIZRA's public documentation and product pages
#      so assistants can accurately ground product answers on verified facts.
#   3. Private user-specific workspaces, authentication flows, admin consoles,
#      and API/WebSocket endpoints (/api/, /live, /ai, /history, /settings,
#      /workspace, /seo-diagnostics, /dashboard, /crawler-audit) are strictly
#      DISALLOWED across every user agent and additionally enforce
#      `noindex, nofollow` and authentication checks server-side.
